## About
Recycl-It is a website that helps people find recycling stations and learn where they can dispose of different types of waste.

## Our goal
We wanted to make recycling easier and more accessible by giving people a simple way to find the right place to dispose of their waste.

## What we did
- Created a simple website
- Added a way to find recycling stations
- Made it easy to search for different types of waste
- Focused on making recycling more convenient
